The assignment I gave myself

Build the horse world a front door without sanding off its personality.

Information lives across show websites, private groups, barn texts, listing pages, and word of mouth. The product needed to organize the chaos while still feeling unmistakably equestrian.

This became a complete brand and product system with responsive navigation, reusable content patterns, marketplace structures, accounts, editable page media, membership, and a voice that can be polished without becoming precious.

Visual direction

Editorial polish with barn dirt still on its boots.

Oxblood creates authority, hunter green keeps the identity grounded, and warm cream gives every feature room to breathe. Strong serif headlines carry culture while the sans serif system handles navigation, forms, and details.

Visual system

Quiet confidence

Structured grids, generous space, equestrian colors, crisp utility, and imagery that never has to shout.

Experience principle

Start with real behavior

The architecture follows what horse people arrive needing: a listing, a show date, a recommendation, a job, or a useful conversation.
