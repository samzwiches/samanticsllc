01

At The In Gate

A digital home for the barn aisle population.

Most equestrian platforms treat the horse world as a collection of listings. At The In Gate was conceived as a living community product shaped around how hunter jumper people actually find information, trade recommendations, plan shows, move horses, and talk.

My roleFounder, strategist, designer, builder
FocusBrand, web, UX, product, community
AudienceRiders, pony families, sellers, shippers, professionals
The challenge

A scattered community with no true front door.

Information lived across Facebook groups, show websites, text threads, word of mouth, and platforms that understood transactions but not culture. The opportunity was to organize the useful stuff without sanding away the personality.

The approach

Build around real barn behavior.

I mapped the platform around show calendars, listings, shippers, directories, membership, community conversations, practical resources, and the specific language that makes horse people recognize one another immediately.

The result

A scalable product with a recognizable soul.

The project became a working platform foundation with clear navigation, reusable content systems, marketplace structures, account flows, and a visual identity that feels polished without pretending the barn is sterile.

02

Pharmarcie

Turning ecommerce confusion into customer confidence.

International fulfillment, split shipments, product questions, tracking, customs, and inventory timing created a support environment where vague language could multiply stress quickly.

My roleCustomer experience and ecommerce contractor
FocusContent, support, knowledge systems, operations
ToolsShopify, Help Scout, Klaviyo, Airtable, email
The challenge

Customers needed answers before they needed apologies.

Important information was spread across systems and often required manual explanation. That increased repeated questions, inconsistent responses, and frustration for both customers and staff.

The approach

Create one calm, useful language system.

I organized recurring questions, wrote help content, clarified fulfillment language, improved Shopify messaging, supported email communication, and documented repeatable response patterns for the team.

The result

Clearer information at every customer touchpoint.

The work created a more consistent support experience, easier internal reference, stronger expectation setting, and a better bridge between customer service and ecommerce operations.

Help center architecture
  • Shipping and tracking
  • Partial orders
  • Returns and cancellations
  • Product and packaging
“Make the answer easy to find before the customer has to ask.”
03

You In Music

A personalized song service built around emotional accuracy.

The product is not merely a song. It is the strange, lovely moment when someone hears their own story returned to them in music.

My roleFounder, brand strategist, experience designer
FocusNaming, product, copy, intake, conversion
MethodHuman storytelling with AI assisted production
The challenge

Personalized products can feel generic fast.

The experience needed to make customers feel understood while collecting enough specific material to create a song with emotional detail, not greeting card wallpaper.

The approach

Design the intake as part of the gift.

I shaped the brand around “putting the you in music,” developed guided story questions, planned the order journey, wrote conversion copy, and connected customer memories to a repeatable creative workflow.

The result

A clear product with genuine emotional stakes.

The concept now has a distinctive name, voice, visual direction, purchase path, and experience model that makes the customer’s story the center of the product.

Core idea
Your story already has a sound. We help you hear it.

Samantics

Making a many brained skill set feel coherent.

Samantics was created to hold the work that does not fit neatly into one job title: creative direction, websites, operations, customer experience, content, documentation, and the ability to see the whole moving machine.

My roleFounder and creative operator
FocusPositioning, services, identity, portfolio
PromiseStructure without flattening personality
The challenge

Variety can look scattered without the right frame.

The brand needed to explain how marketing, systems, websites, writing, operations, and customer thinking belong together rather than presenting them as unrelated services.

The approach

Name the common thread: useful clarity.

I positioned the practice as creative operations, built a voice that is warm and direct, organized the services around business outcomes, and created a visual identity that balances polish with real life texture.

The result

A brand broad enough to be honest and clear enough to hire.

Samantics now functions as a coherent professional home for strategy, digital work, content, customer experience, and operational problem solving.

How I work

Find the knot. Pull the right thread. Do not set the sweater on fire.

  1. 01

    Notice

    I look for where people are confused, where work repeats, where the brand goes quiet, and where the system is quietly eating everyone’s afternoon.

  2. 02

    Make meaning

    I connect the audience, the business goal, the content, and the operational reality so we solve the actual problem instead of applying decorative wallpaper.

  3. 03

    Build clearly

    I create the words, structure, design direction, documentation, or workflow with enough order to use and enough life to remember.

  4. 04

    Leave it usable

    The final work should make the next step easier for customers and for the people who have to maintain it after launch.
