Good to know

A few things before we open the case.

Do you work on Macs?

Some Mac troubleshooting and selected repairs can be assessed individually. Certain parts require manufacturer tools, calibration, or specialist equipment, so not every Mac repair will be available.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the device and the actual problem. Reach out with your model and symptoms for next steps. Costs and parts are discussed before work begins.

What about my files and privacy?

Your information deserves respect. Back up important files before any repair whenever possible. Some repairs carry a risk of data loss, and data recovery cannot be guaranteed. Never send account passwords in your initial message.

Can you help remotely?

For some software and setup issues, yes, depending on the problem and your device. Hardware repairs and physical inspections require an in person arrangement.

Have a stubborn machine?

Tell me what it's doing.
Especially the weird part.

Include your computer make and model, a short description of the issue, and whether you're in the Northern Kentucky or Cincinnati area. I'll help you figure out the next sensible step.
